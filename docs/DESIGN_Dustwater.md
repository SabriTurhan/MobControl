# Dustwater: Squad Command — design

Base: Watermelon "Epic Runner" (lane runner, squad auto-shoots gates/barrels/enemies).
Goal: hybrid-casual depth = **A) town to liberate & build** + **C) commander card collection with squad selection**,
a short story, and an intro cinematic. Art stays in the template language (low-poly, flat palette-gradient
atlases, `WMelon/Universal` shader, Fredoka UI, chunky rounded panels).

## Story
Dustwater is a small desert trading town. The **Red Scorpions** (red bandits — the template's red soldiers)
led by **Boss Vex** took it over. You are the **Commander** of the **Blue Rangers** (blue soldiers).
Liberate the town district by district, rebuild it, and kick Vex out.

| Chapter | Levels | District | Boss (last level) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Outskirts | 1–10 | gas station, water tower | Lieutenant **Rattler** |
| 2 Main Street | 11–20 | saloon, market | **Big Jaws** (heavy) |
| 3 Scorpion Fort | 21–30 | bandit fort | **Boss Vex** |
| Patrols | 31+ | random replays with scaled HP | – |

Intro cinematic (first launch, skippable): peaceful town → bandit convoy arrives, scorpion flags rise →
Vex close-up → blue squad on the hill: "Commander, take Dustwater back!" → town screen.
Short story beats (1–2 lines) also play when a chapter starts and when a boss falls.

## Core loop
Town screen → **Operation briefing** (intel + choose squad cards + weapon card) → run → chest (card) →
rewards (Money + Supplies + card copies) → build/upgrade buildings, level up cards → next operation.

## C) Commander cards
Two kinds. Card level 1–10, each level ≈ +12% of the unit's main stats; level-up costs copies + Money.

**Unit cards** (each selected card = one soldier at the start; gates still add plain Rangers):
| Card | Rarity | Role | Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ranger | common | balanced, uses the squad weapon | blue soldier (template) |
| Heavy Gunner | rare | 2× HP, locked minigun | big soldier (template) |
| Sharpshooter | rare | long range, high damage, slow, bullets pierce | NEW: soldier re-dye + sniper rifle + cap |
| Medic | rare | heals the squad over time, weak pistol | NEW: white/red re-dye + medic bag |
| Grenadier | epic | lobbed grenades, splash damage, great vs. barricades/crowds | NEW: olive re-dye + grenade launcher + helmet |

**Weapon cards** (squad weapon of all Rangers at the start; weapon gates still swap it during a run):
Pistol (common), Shotgun (common), Rifle (rare), Minigun (epic).

Squad slots come from the Command Post level (2 → 5).
Sources of cards: level chest (1 card), chapter boss (guaranteed rare+), building milestones, store.

## Level design driven by the squad
Each level has **Intel** (shown on the briefing) — 1–2 threat tags, each with a counter:
| Threat | Content | Counters |
|---|---|---|
| Armored | Heavy bandits, high-HP | Sharpshooter, Rifle |
| Swarm | many weak bandits close together | Minigun, Grenadier |
| Snipers | NEW Bandit Sniper, out-ranges the squad | Sharpshooter, range gates |
| Barricades | NEW sandbag walls, high HP, block lanes | Grenadier, Shotgun |
| Attrition | lots of incoming fire over a long road | Medic, Heavy Gunner |

The briefing shows the threats and highlights owned cards that counter them; it never forces a pick.
Boss levels end with a boss before the finish line (big HP bar, shoots bursts).

## A) Town of Dustwater
A 3D diorama (same art style) shown behind the main menu. Plots unlock as chapters are liberated.
Buildings cost **Money + Supplies** (Supplies = new currency, dropped by supply crates/barricades in levels).
| Building | Unlocks | Bonus per level (max 5) |
|---|---|---|
| Command Post | start | squad slots 2/3/4/5/5 + card level cap |
| Barracks | ch.1 L3 | +10% squad HP (replaces the old Health upgrade) |
| Armory | ch.1 L5 | +10% damage (replaces Damage upgrade) |
| Workshop | ch.1 L8 | +8% fire rate (replaces Fire Rate upgrade) |
| Field Hospital | ch.2 | 1 free revive per run, + healing over time |
| Saloon | ch.2 | idle Money income per hour (collect) |
| Watchtower | ch.3 | +range, better chest odds |
Liberated districts swap scorpion flags for blue flags and ruined props for repaired ones.

## Art plan (procedural, template palette)
- New meshes generated by editor code, UV'd into the existing palette atlases (flat gradient bands),
  rendered with `WMelon/Universal` materials already in the project.
- Units: re-dyed soldier skinned meshes (atlas band remap) + small attached props.
- Buildings: adobe houses, saloon, water tower, gas station, tents, watchtower, fences, flags, sandbags,
  bandit truck. Two states: ruined/occupied and rebuilt.
- Card portraits and building icons rendered from the 3D models into sprites (same as template icons).

## Built (2026-10-18)
- Code: `Assets/Project Files/Game/Scripts/Campaign/` (Campaign state, cards, squad, buildings, rewards, town view,
  intro, boss, sniper sight, medic, runtime UI in `UI/`), dev bot in `Campaign/Dev/DustwaterPlayBot.cs`.
- Builders (menu **Tools/Dustwater**, all re-runnable, run in this order after art changes):
  1. Art/Build Units, Weapons, Props (also paints soldier dyes + palette)
  2. Art/Build Town (pieces, road environments, diorama in Game scene)
  3. Art/Render Portraits + Icons
  4. Content/Register Content + Campaign Database (guns, characters, enemies, obstacles, Supplies, UI style)
  5. Content/Build 30 Missions
  6. Content/Build Intro Cinematic
- Template changes: gun pierce/splash/aim/unblockable, AdvanceMultiplier (boss standoff), stats from buildings,
  squad spawn from cards, Supplies currency, free revive, "MISSION N" label, upgrades panel/Play hidden by the hub.
- Balance (bot, expected progression): 30/30 missions won by the bot after tuning; bosses: Rattler 380 hp,
  Jaws 520 hp, Vex 6000 hp. Enemy shots: snipers, bosses and ambush rifles aim at the squad; sniper and boss
  shots are not cancelled by the squad's bullets.

## Phases
1. Card + squad data, save, squad spawn, new unit behaviours (pierce, heal, splash), Supplies currency.
2. New art: units, weapons, bandit sniper, bosses, sandbags, supply crate, town buildings.
3. UI: briefing/squad screen, card collection, town screen with buildings.
4. Levels 1–30 with intel tags, bosses, story beats; balance pass with a play bot.
5. Intro cinematic.
